## Who we are

Lost Souls Interactive (LSI) is composed of 9 developers from all over the world. We've banded together as artists to create an experience like no other.

## Mission

Our mission is to contribute to the new wave of digital artists that blend art, history, and technical skills into our favorite medium for telling stories and entertaining.

## Values

We value the era of gaming where you could sit down, play a game, not be bothered, and not ever have to ask the question if the money spent was worth it. We never used to wait for sales to get our favorite games, we waited outside stores on opening night.

## What makes us unique

As a studio, our distance from one another makes us far different from most. Throughout the development of Hotel Espir alone, many of us have experienced loss. To start with, our first programmer went away to climb in the Italian alps, and we never heard from him again. While we don't know exactly what happened to him, it never felt like he left us, but never came back. Since then, losing people we know and love has plagued our studio, making the distance between us feel all the more lonely.
